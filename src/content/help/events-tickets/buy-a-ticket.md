## Buying a ticket

1. Open **Events** and choose a ticketed event.
2. Select **Get tickets**.
3. Choose the **Number of tickets** and your **Price per ticket**.
4. Select **Pay** and complete the secure card checkout.

Ticket prices are a suggestion: pay what you can, down to the event's minimum if
it has one. As a signed-in member, your name and email are filled in for you.

For a free event, the button is **Get free ticket** and there is no checkout.

## After purchase

Your ticket appears under **My tickets** on the Events page as a stub. When you
arrive, [show your ticket's QR code](/member/help/ticket-check-in) to check in.

A confirmation is also sent to your email.

## Buying without an account

Anyone can buy tickets as a guest from the public events page — an account isn't
required — but signing in keeps all your tickets in one place.
