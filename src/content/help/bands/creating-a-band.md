## Creating a band

1. In the sidebar, choose **New** beside **My bands**.
2. Give it a **Band name** and, if you like, a short **Bio**.
3. Choose **Create band** — you become the band's owner and its dashboard opens.

## Next steps

Once the band exists, from its dashboard you can:

- [Invite members](/member/help/invite-band-members) (existing members or by email).
- [Flesh out the band profile](/member/help/edit-band-profile) — genres, links,
  and visibility.
- [Reserve practice space](/member/help/book-band-session) for the band.

## Roles

- **Owner** — Full control, including deleting the band or transferring ownership.
- **Admin** — Can edit the band profile, its events, and its members.
- **Member** — Can see the schedule and band info, and book for the band.

See [managing members](/member/help/manage-band-members) for how roles work in
practice.
