## Editing the profile

Owners and admins edit the band's public listing from **Edit profile** in the
band's sidebar. It works much like your personal profile.

## What you can set

- **Band name, Tagline & Avatar** — The band's identity on its directory card.
- **Hometown** and **Founded** — Shown as "Based in" and "Formed" on the profile.
- **Bio** — A rich-text description.
- **Genres** — Freeform tags (with suggestions) used in directory filters.
- **We're looking for members** — Flag the band as open to new members, and
  list the **Roles wanted**.
- **Links** — SoundCloud, YouTube, Spotify, and more; those three show as
  embedded players.
- **Enquiry contacts** — An **Email**, **Phone** and **Social handle** for
  enquiries. They are never shown publicly: the band's page offers a contact form
  instead, and messages sent through it land here.
- **Directory visibility** — **Hidden**, **Members only** or **Public**.

Choose **Save** when you're done. For a richer public site, see
[Premium band pages](/member/help/premium-band-pages).
