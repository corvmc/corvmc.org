## My bands

Every band you're part of is listed under **My bands** in the sidebar. The
**My bands** page lists them too, with any pending invitations; **Answer** on an
invitation in your **Dashboard** opens it.

## On the page

- **Pending invitations** — Bands that have invited you appear here to
  [accept or decline](/member/help/accept-band-invitation).
- **Your bands** — A card for each band you belong to, with its member count and
  your role. Click one to open its dashboard.
- **Create band** — Start a new band of your own. **New** beside **My bands** in
  the sidebar does the same.
