## Booking for the band

Any member of the band can book practice sessions in the band's name.

1. Open the band's **Reservations** page and choose **Book a Session**.
2. Pick a **date**, a **start time**, and an **end time**.
3. Add **notes** if you need to.
4. Review the cost and confirm.

The session belongs to the band and shows up on the band dashboard for everyone
in it.

## Whose free hours get used

Bands don't have free hours of their own. Whoever books spends **their own**
personal free hours, and the confirmation screen says so before you commit —
so two bandmates can see different prices for the same slot, and that's why.
Anything left over is due at the door.

## Recurring band sessions

If your band practices on a schedule, choose a recurrence (weekly, bi-weekly, or
monthly) when booking. Recurring bookings are a
[sustaining-member benefit](/member/help/recurring-reservations), and at least
one active member of the band needs a sustaining membership.

## Cancelling

Whoever booked a session can cancel it. Band **owners and admins** can cancel
any of the band's sessions. If you don't see a Cancel button on a booking,
someone else made it — ask them, or ask an admin.
