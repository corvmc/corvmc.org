## Inviting members

From the band's **Members** page (owners and admins), choose **Invite members**
and add everyone you want to invite before sending:

- **Existing members** — Type a name in **People** and pick it from the list.
  When only one member matches, pressing Enter adds them.
- **By email** — Type the address of someone without an account and press Enter.
  They get an email, and signing up with that address puts them in the band.

The **Role** and **Position** you choose apply to everyone on the list. Choose
**Send invitations** when the list is ready. Each person gets
their own invitation. If one can't be sent — they are already in the band, for
example — the others still go out, and the modal lists who was not invited and
why.

## What happens next

- Invited members appear at the top of the **Members** list, marked
  **Pending**, until they accept. So do people invited by email.
- Members [accept from their My bands page](/member/help/accept-band-invitation).
  People invited by email join when they sign up.

## Managing invitations

You can **Revoke** a pending invitation any time from the **Members** page. See
[managing members](/member/help/manage-band-members) for roles and removals.
