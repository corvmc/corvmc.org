## Links

In the **Links** section of your **Profile** page, add links to your music and online presence — YouTube,
Spotify, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Instagram, a personal site, and so on. These show
on your profile so people can hear and follow you. Add as many as are relevant.

## Directory contact info

Separately, **Directory contact info** takes a **Display email**, **Phone** and
**Social handle** that other members can use to reach you directly.

This is optional and independent of your account email. By default only
signed-in members see it; tick **Show my contact info on my public profile** to
show it to everyone. Use [visibility settings](/member/help/directory-visibility)
to control who can see your profile at all.
