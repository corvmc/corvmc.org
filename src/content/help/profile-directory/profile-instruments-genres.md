## Instruments

In the **Music** section of your **Profile** page, add the instruments you play
under **Instruments**. As you type, suggestions
appear — pick an existing one to stay consistent with how others are tagged, or
add your own. List everything you'd want to be found for.

## Genres

Add **Genres** the same way. These describe the styles you play or are
interested in. **Skills** is for what you can help with besides playing — sound
engineering, photography, promotion.
Genres and instruments are what other members filter by in the directory, so the
more accurate they are, the better your matches.

## Why it matters

Members searching the [directory](/member/help/find-members-bands) can narrow by
instrument, skill and genre. Good tags are the difference between being found and being
missed.
