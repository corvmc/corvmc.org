## What it is for

**Classifieds** in the member panel is a board of short posts, each one either
**Wanted** or **Offered**: a band looking for a bassist, a keys player free for
fill-ins, an open jam on Thursday, someone who can mix your EP.

The directory tells you who plays what. A post says what somebody needs right
now, in their own words.

## Posting

Press **New post**, choose Wanted or Offered and a category, then write a title and
the details.

- **Tags** for instruments, genres and skills make your post easier to find.
  Anyone who clicks a tag sees every post that shares it.
- **Post as** appears if you own or run a band. The post then shows the band as
  well as you.
- You can have up to five open posts at a time.

## How long a post lasts

A post stays up for **30 days**. Press **Renew** on your post to give it another
30 days from today. Once it has done its job, press **Close**. Closed and expired
posts leave the board, but you can still find them under **Your posts**.

## Answering a post

Press **Message** on the post to send its author a message request. If messaging
is not available, the post points you to the author's profile, which shows how to
reach them.

## Reports

If a post breaks the rules, use the flag on it. A reported post comes off the
board while staff look. If staff agree, it stays down, and the author sees a note
saying what to change. When the author edits it, it goes back to staff for
another look. Posts are never deleted by moderation.
