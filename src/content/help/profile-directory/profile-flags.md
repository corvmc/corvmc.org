## Status flags

The **Music** section of your profile tells other members what you're open to.

**Looking for** has three choices: **Nothing right now**, **A band to join**, or
**Members for my project** — which adds **Instruments you need**. Your dashboard
suggests bands and members to meet from this.

Then tick the ones that apply:

- **I'm available for hire** — You'll take paid gigs or studio work.
- **I teach lessons privately** — You offer instruction. Anywhere: your front room, over a video call,
  another town. It is something you say about yourself and nobody checks it.

  This is **not** the same as teaching at the Collective. That is a separate thing staff grant,
  it lets you book the room at the member rate without the monthly cap, and it puts you on the
  [teacher directory](/directory/instructors). Apply for it from **Teaching at CMC** on your
  profile. You can have either, both, or neither.

- **I'm open to collaboration** — You're up for one-off or project-based work.

## How they're used

These flags appear on your directory card and help others quickly see how to work
with you. They are also the directory's filters — **Looking for band**,
**Available for hire**, **Teaches privately** and **Open to collaboration** — so
members looking specifically for, say, a teacher or a hired player can find you.

Set them on your **Profile** page and save. You can change them any time as your
situation changes.
