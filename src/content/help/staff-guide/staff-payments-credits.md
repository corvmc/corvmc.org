## Payments

**Staff → Money → Payments** is the record of cash and credit-settled payments; card
payments (tickets, online reservation payments) are in the Stripe dashboard. Search by
member and filter by payment method, status, and date range. Each row shows the member,
amount, method, status, the date paid, the **Record** id to copy, and **View reservation**.

## Credits

**Staff → Money → Credit transactions** is the ledger of credit activity — practice credits
and equipment credits. Search by member and filter by credit type and source to see
where credits came from and how they were used.

## Reconciling

- Use payments to confirm a cash or credit payment; look up card charges in Stripe.
- Use the credit ledger to verify a member's balance or investigate a discrepancy.
- Adjust an individual member's balance from their
  [user detail page](/member/help/staff-edit-user).
