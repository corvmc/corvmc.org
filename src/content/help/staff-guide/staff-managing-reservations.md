## The reservations list

**Staff → Practice space → Reservations** lists every booking and closure, grouped by day.
One row of filters narrows it:

- **Booker type** and search: pick Members, Groups, Productions, Events or Closures, and
  search by member name, email, band or event title.
- **Date**: From today (the default), Next 7 days, Past 30 days, Any date, or your own
  From and To dates.
- **Status**: Waitlisted, Booked, Paid, Payment due, Covered, No-show, Cancelled or
  Refunded. Each option shows how many bookings it would list.

The filters are kept in the address, so going back from a booking returns to the same
view.

## Payment due

**Payment due** lists reservations that ended with money still owed; an upcoming booking not
yet paid reads **Booked**. Choosing it sets the date to Any date. A red dot on the Status
filter means at least one ended reservation still owes money.

A reservation that ended owing money shows **Record cash payment** and **No-show** on its row.

## Actions

- **Complete**: mark a reservation done after it happens. It is on the row once the reservation
  has ended.
- **No-show**: flag when the member did not arrive.
- **Cancel reservation**: cancel with an optional reason.

Open any row to reach the [detail page](/member/help/staff-reservation-payments), which
carries these actions, payment handling and a day-timeline view of the slot.

## Building access

**Building access** is a tab of **Settings** in the **Staff → Practice space** header, and is
about the door. When the last reading found the lock offline, **Lock offline** shows in the
Reservations header and opens it.

- **Lock status**: online or offline, and when the lock was last seen.
- **Active codes**: per-booking codes, key holders' standing codes and the break-glass
  code. Each shows how it ends and whether it is on the lock or waiting for it.
- **Key holders**: who holds a physical key or standing code. **Record a key or code** adds
  one; **Mark returned** closes it.
- **Lock settings**: the U-tec connection, lock health and the last daily run, with **Test
  connection**, **Run lock self-test**, **Rotate** for the break-glass code and **Reconnect**.

## Practice space settings

**Settings** in the header opens the space's own settings: **Pricing**, **Booking rules**,
**Operating hours**, **The room** and **Outstanding fees**. New-member orientation is on
**Settings** in the **Staff → Volunteering** header.

## Closures

**Add closure** is in the menu beside **New reservation**. Closures show in the list under
All bookers or the **Closures** booker type, with All statuses.
