## The reservations list

**Staff → Practice space** lists every booking and closure, grouped by day. One row of
filters narrows it:

- **Booker type** and search: pick Members, Groups, Productions or Events, and search by
  member name, email, band or event title.
- **Date**: From today (the default), Next 7 days, Past 30 days, Any date, or your own
  From and To dates.
- **Status**: Confirmed, Payment due, Waitlisted or Cancelled. Each option shows how many
  bookings it would list.

The filters are kept in the address, so going back from a booking returns to the same
view.

## Payment due

**Payment due** lists sessions that ended with money still owed, plus upcoming confirmed
bookings not yet paid. Choosing it sets the date to Any date. A red dot on the Status
filter means at least one ended session still owes money.

A session that ended owing money shows **Cash received** and **No-show** on its row.

## Actions

- **Confirm**: approve a waitlisted reservation.
- **Complete**: mark a session done after it happens.
- **No-show**: flag when the member did not arrive.
- **Cancel**: cancel with an optional reason.

Open any row to reach the [detail page](/member/help/staff-reservation-payments) for
payment handling and a day-timeline view of the slot.

## Building access

**Building access** in the header opens a sidebar about the door. A red dot on the button
means the last reading found the lock offline.

- **Lock status**: online or offline, and when the lock was last seen.
- **Active codes**: per-booking codes, key holders' standing codes and the break-glass
  code. Each shows its last two digits and whether it is on the lock or waiting for it.
- **Lock settings**: the U-tec connection, lock health and the last daily run, with Test
  connection, Run self-test, Rotate break-glass code and Reconnect.

## Practice space settings

**Settings** in the header opens the space's own settings: pricing, the hourly rate,
operating hours, booking rules, the room's console channels and orientation.

## Closures

**Add closure** is in the menu beside **New reservation**. Closures show in the list under
All bookers and All statuses.
