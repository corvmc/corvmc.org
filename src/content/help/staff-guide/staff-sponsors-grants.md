Both live on one list: **Staff → Partners → Funding**. The funders and sponsors themselves are
organizations under **Contacts**.

Staff can add and change both, and so can members of any committee whose grants include them.
The Development Committee has them to start with. The treasurer can read both. A committee member
who holds no staff position still gets a **Staff** tab, which opens on the lists their committee
can use, and they receive the deadline reminders.

To change which committee manages these, open the committee under **Staff → People → Groups**
and edit its **Grants**.

## The Funding page

**Funding** groups every grant application and sponsorship by stage: prospects, pending
applications, active funding, and (behind **Show closed**) closed. Within a stage, whatever needs
something soonest is at the top: its next task or its own deadline, whichever comes first.

Each grant application and each sponsorship has a **project** of its own, made with it and owned by
the Development Committee. The work goes there as tasks: the steps of an application, the reports
an award requires, a call to the program officer. Tasks have due dates and people assigned, show on
the assignee's schedule, and are reminded like any committee task. The Funding page shows each
row's next open task.

## Sponsors

Add the business under **Contacts** as an organization with the **Sponsor** role. On **Funding**,
choose **Add sponsorship** and pick the sponsor: a title, a tier, the amount, and the dates it runs.
A sponsor keeps its whole history, so next season is a new sponsorship on the same sponsor.

| Status   | Means                 |
| -------- | --------------------- |
| Pitched  | Asked, not yet agreed |
| Active   | Agreed and running    |
| Ended    | Ran its course        |
| Declined | Said no               |

When an active term's end date passes, it shows in red and the sponsor's page says so until you
mark it Ended or add the renewal.

### Crediting a sponsor on a show

On the sponsor's page, choose **Place** beside a sponsorship. Pick the show, then choose where to
credit the sponsor:

- **Event page:** the public event page shows "Sponsored by" and the sponsor's
  logo or name, linked to their website.
- **Email:** every campaign about that show ends with the same line.

Only an Active or Ended sponsorship is credited. You can place a pitch ahead of signing, and it
appears once you mark it Active. Upload the sponsor's **Logo** on the same page. Without one,
credits show the name.

Removing a placement takes the credit off the event page right away. Emails that were already
sent keep their credit.

## Grants

Add the funder first under **Funders**, then choose **New application** on the Funding page. The
application opens with its first tasks already on its project. **Applying for a grant** walks
through one grant from start to finish.

| Status   | Means                        | Deadline shown |
| -------- | ---------------------------- | -------------- |
| Prospect | Not yet applied              | Apply by       |
| Applied  | Waiting on the funder        | none           |
| Awarded  | Money awarded, award running | Award end      |
| Declined | Turned down                  | none           |
| Closed   | Finished                     | none           |

Keep **Requested** and **Awarded** separate: funders often award less than was asked.

A report the funder requires is a task on the grant's project, due on the funder's date. Mark it
done when you submit the report. A closed grant with a report still open stays on the Funding page.

## Funded project

A grant or sponsorship can name the **funded project**: the project the money pays for, such as a
repair fair or a room refit. That project's budget then lists it under **Grants and sponsorships**,
beside what the project has spent. Leave it as **General support** for operating money.

## Reminders

Anyone who manages grants or sponsors is notified two weeks before a deadline and again three days
before. That covers an application's apply-by date, the end of an award, and the end of an active
sponsorship. The staff dashboard lists deadlines in the next 30 days, and overdue ones in red.

Tasks on a grant's or sponsorship's project, reports included, are reminded two weeks ahead, three
days ahead, and once overdue. The reminder goes to whoever is assigned, or to every member of the
Development Committee while nobody is.

If you change a deadline's date, the reminders go out again for the new date. Turn the email off
under your notification settings, as **Grant or sponsorship deadline**.

## What this does not do

It does not put logos on posters, and it does not take or record money. A cheque that arrives is entered
in the books the usual way.
