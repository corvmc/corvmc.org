Both live under **Staff → Money**, as two separate lists.

## Sponsors

**Sponsors** lists every business that sponsors the collective, with whichever term ends soonest
at the top.

Choose **New sponsor** and give the business a name. Open it to add a **sponsorship**: a title,
a tier, the amount, and the dates it runs. A sponsor keeps its whole history, so next season is a
new sponsorship on the same sponsor.

| Status   | Means                 |
| -------- | --------------------- |
| Pitched  | Asked, not yet agreed |
| Active   | Agreed and running    |
| Ended    | Ran its course        |
| Declined | Said no               |

When an active term's end date passes, it shows in red and the sponsor's page says so until you
mark it Ended or add the renewal.

## Grants

**Grants** lists applications, with whatever comes due soonest at the top. Add the funder first
under **Funders**, then choose **New application**.

| Status   | Means                        | Next deadline shown            |
| -------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Prospect | Not yet applied              | Apply by                       |
| Applied  | Waiting on the funder        | none                           |
| Awarded  | Money awarded, award running | First report due, or award end |
| Declined | Turned down                  | none                           |
| Closed   | Finished                     | Any report still not submitted |

Keep **Requested** and **Awarded** separate: funders often award less than was asked.

Open an awarded grant to add each **report** the funder requires, with its due date. When you send
one, edit it and fill in **Submitted**. A report that is past due shows in red until it has a
submitted date, even after the grant is closed.

## What this does not do

It does not send reminders, and it does not take or record money. A cheque that arrives is entered
in the books the usual way.
