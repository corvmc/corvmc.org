A club or committee has no single owner. It is run by its **chairs**: any number of members,
including none. A program with no chairs keeps running; it just has nobody on the member side to
manage it until staff appoint one.

Chairs can manage the roster (invite, remove, set each member's role and position), write
announcements, upload documents, schedule sessions and answer applications. Members cannot set
their own position; a chair or staff does that.

## Add a chair

Open the club under **Staff → People → Clubs**, or the committee under **Staff → Planning →
Committees**.

- **Someone already on the roster:** choose **Make chair** on their row.
- **Anyone else:** choose **Add chair** at the top of the page and search for the member. They are
  put on the roster as a chair straight away, with nothing to accept.

You can also pick a first chair when you create the club or committee.

## Remove a chair

Choose **Remove chair** on their row. They stay on the roster as an ordinary member. To take them
off the roster entirely, a chair removes them from the group's own page.

Every change is recorded in the audit log.

Bands are different: a band has exactly one owner, who hands it on themselves.
