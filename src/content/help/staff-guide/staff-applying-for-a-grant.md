This walks through one grant from the first look to the final report. The app adds the tasks for
each stage, each with a note on what it means. You do the work and tick the tasks off.

Everything here happens on **Staff → Partners → Funding**, on the grant's page, and on the grant's
project, which holds its tasks. **Track Sponsors and Grants** covers the lists and the statuses.

## 1. Add the funder and the application

1. Under **Funders**, add the foundation or agency if it is not there yet. Add the program officer
   as its contact if you know who that is.
2. On **Funding**, choose **New application**. Give it a name you will recognise, such as "2027
   operating support", and set **Apply by** to the funder's deadline.
3. Leave the status as **Prospect**.

The application opens with six tasks on its project, timed back from the apply-by date:

| Task                       | Due            | What it means                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------- | -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Read the guidelines        | 6 weeks before | The deadline, the amount range, what the money may pay for, and every question the application asks.              |
| Check eligibility          | 5 weeks before | Nonprofit status, location, the kind of work funded, any match required. Ask the program officer when unsure.     |
| Draft the narrative        | 3 weeks before | The need, what CMC will do, who it serves, and how you will know it worked, in the funder's order.                |
| Build the budget           | 3 weeks before | What the grant pays for, line by line, and the project's other income, donated goods and volunteer time included. |
| Collect letters of support | 2 weeks before | Short letters from partners or community members saying why the work matters. Ask three weeks ahead.              |
| Submit the application     | The day before | Submit, keep a copy of everything sent and the confirmation, then set the status to **Applied**.                  |

Assign each task to whoever is doing it. It then shows on their schedule and they are reminded
two weeks ahead, three days ahead, and once it is overdue. A task nobody holds reminds the whole
Development Committee.

To add a step of your own, such as "Call the program officer", add a task on the grant's project.

## 2. Wait for the decision

Set the status to **Applied** once it is submitted. Nothing comes due while the funder decides.
When they answer, set **Awarded** with the amount, or **Declined**.

## 3. The award

Set the status to **Awarded**, the **Awarded** amount, and the award period. The grant's page then
offers **Add award tasks**. Choose it to add:

- **Sign the grant agreement**: read it for the report dates, the spending rules and how to credit
  the funder. A board officer signs it.
- **Add a task for each report**: one task per report the agreement requires, due on the funder's
  date. Mark each done when the report is submitted.
- **Set the funded project**: edit the grant and pick the project the money pays for, so that
  project's budget shows the award. Leave it as **General support** for operating money.

## 4. Close-out

A month before the award ends, the grant's page offers **Add close-out tasks**:

- **Send the thank-you letter**, with a photo or a few words from someone the work reached.
- **Submit the final report**: what was done, who it reached, and how the money was spent against
  the application's budget. The agreement says when; it is often 30 to 60 days after the award
  ends.

Set the status to **Closed** once the final report is in.

## When something is off

- **The grant has no tasks.** The template may be switched off under **Staff → Planning → Tasks →
  Templates**. Apply **Grant application**, **Grant award** or **Grant close-out** from the grant's
  project instead.
- **The tasks have no dates.** The grant had no apply-by date, award start or award end when they
  were added. Set the date on the grant and they are placed.
