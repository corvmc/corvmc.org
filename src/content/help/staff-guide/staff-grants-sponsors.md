**Staff → Money → Grants & sponsors** lists every grant and sponsorship the collective has open,
with whatever comes due soonest at the top.

## Add one

Choose **New agreement**. Only the kind, who it is with, and a title are required. Fill in the
dates you know:

- **Apply by**: the application deadline, while it is still a prospect.
- **Starts** and **Ends**: the award period or the sponsorship term.
- **Report due**: when the funder expects a report.

**Amount** is what you asked for until it is awarded, and then what was awarded.

## Move it along

Open the agreement and choose **Edit** to change its status:

| Status   | Means                              | Next deadline shown |
| -------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Prospect | Not yet applied, or not yet signed | Apply by            |
| Applied  | Waiting on the funder's decision   | none                |
| Active   | Awarded or signed, and running     | Report due or Ends  |
| Declined | Turned down                        | none                |
| Ended    | Finished, with the report in       | none                |

When a deadline has passed it shows in red, and the agreement's page says so until the status
moves on. Declined and ended agreements drop off the list. Tick **Show closed** to see them again.

## What this does not do

It does not send reminders, and it does not take or record money. A grant cheque that arrives is
entered in the books the usual way.
