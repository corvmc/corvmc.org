## System

**Staff → System** holds the settings that span the whole site. It opens its own sidebar;
changes are validated and saved per page. Some settings are admin-only.

## The System pages

- **Organization** — Location, contact email and social links.
- **Features** — Which features are switched on.
- **Access** — Admins only. Who holds which position, committee grant and clearance.
  **By person** shows one member's access; **By permission** lists everyone holding a
  position, capability or clearance. Positions are assigned and removed here.
- **Audit log** — Who changed what, and when.

## Settings that live with their module

- Pricing, booking rules, operating hours and the room are on **Practice space settings**:
  **Settings** in the **Staff → Practice space** header.
- The smart-lock (U-tec) settings are under **Lock settings** on
  **Staff → Practice space → Building access**.
- The volunteer hour value and new-member orientation are on **Volunteering settings**:
  **Settings** in the **Staff → Volunteering** header.
- **Channels** in the **Staff → Inbox** toolbar configures the email, SMS, web, Instagram,
  and Messenger channels that feed the [inbox](/member/help/staff-inbox).

## Maintenance actions

**Sync subscriptions** and **Refresh stats** are on **Staff → Members**.

## Caution

These settings affect the whole system — rates, booking rules, and live
integrations. Change them deliberately and test integration connections after
editing keys.
