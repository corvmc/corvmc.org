## The staff area

The **Staff** panel is where you run day-to-day operations. The dashboard shows your tasks,
everything waiting for your review, today's and tomorrow's room bookings, upcoming shows,
short-staffed shifts, deadlines, low stock and recent members. Each section appears only if you
can act on it.

## Your tasks

**Your tasks** lists the open tasks waiting on you: the ones you are assigned to and have not
marked done, and the unassigned ones belonging to a committee you sit on. Overdue tasks come
first, then the rest by date, then those with no date. A task about a record, such as an amp or
a booking, opens that record when you can open it; any other task opens its own page. The section
is hidden when nothing is waiting.

Anyone on a task's committee may take it, tick its checklist, mark it done and hand it back, as
may its assignee. Editing, assigning someone else or calling the task off needs the volunteer
coordinator's permission.

## Waiting for review

**Waiting for review** lists everything that needs a staff decision, oldest first: suggestions,
events, classified posts, resource tips, content reports and appeals, reported problems,
group and vendor applications, volunteer hours, shift claims and volunteers under 18. You see only
the kinds your role can decide. The buttons above the list narrow it to one kind and show how many
of each are waiting.

Some rows can be decided in place:

- **Approve** decides the row in one click.
- **Decline** asks for a reason first when the member is told why, as for events, posts, tips
  and hours.

A decided row leaves the list, and the Dashboard badge counts one fewer. Content reports, appeals,
applications and suggestions that need an answer open their own page, because the decision there
needs a written reply or more than two choices.

## What's in the sidebar

Two rows sit on their own at the top:

- **Dashboard** — The view described above. The badge counts everything waiting for your review.
- **Inbox** — Contact-form and channel messages. The badge counts unresolved threads.

Everything else is grouped by what it's for:

- **Relationships** — **Contacts** (outside organizations, with funders, sponsors and
  contractors among them), **Outreach** (**Campaigns**, **Audiences** and **Posters**) and
  **Funding** (grant applications and sponsorships).
- **Community** — **Members** (member accounts, roles, and credits; volunteers and teachers are
  role filters), **Bands**, **Suggestions**, **Ballots**, **Moderation** (**Content flags**,
  **Incidents** and **Blocklist**), **Classifieds** and **Content** (**Help articles** and
  **Local resources**).
- **Programs** — **Events** (creating and running events, ticketing and check-in, with
  productions, venues and vendor applications as views), **Practice space** (**Reservations**,
  **Recurring series** and **Building access**), **Clubs** and **Music**.
- **Operations** — **Tasks**, **Committees**, **Volunteering**, **Equipment**, **Supplies** and
  **Money** (**Payments** and **Credit transactions**). Every task, in a project or not, is on
  **Tasks**, with **Projects** beside it and **Recurring work** and **Templates** in its header.
  A task can wait on others: set it on the task's page under **Waits on**. It shows as
  **Blocked** on the table until they are done, and the project's **Task graph** draws the
  order. Volunteer shifts are **Volunteering**, whose badge counts understaffed shifts in the
  next seven days and tasks with ended shifts.

Below the groups, **Reports** holds the annual, volunteering, inventory spend and music
reports, and **System** opens the site-wide settings: **Organization**, **Features**,
**Access** and **Audit log**.

Click a group's heading to collapse it, and the sidebar remembers your choice next time. Opening a
page inside a group you collapsed re-opens that group, so you can always see where you are.

Most lists support search and filtering; click a row to open its detail page.
