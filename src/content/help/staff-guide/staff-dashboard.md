## The staff area

The **Staff** panel is where you run day-to-day operations. The dashboard shows today's and
tomorrow's room bookings, upcoming shows, everything waiting for your review, short-staffed
shifts, deadlines, low stock and recent members. Each section appears only if you can act on it.

## Waiting for review

**Waiting for review** lists everything that needs a staff decision, oldest first: suggestions,
event listings, classified posts, resource tips, content reports and appeals, reported problems,
group and vendor applications, volunteer hours, shift claims and volunteers under 18. You see only
the kinds your role can decide. The buttons above the list narrow it to one kind and show how many
of each are waiting.

Some rows can be decided in place:

- **Approve** decides the row in one click.
- **Decline** asks for a reason first when the member is told why, as for listings, posts, tips
  and hours.

A decided row leaves the list, and the Dashboard badge counts one fewer. Content reports, appeals,
applications and suggestions that need an answer open their own page, because the decision there
needs a written reply or more than two choices.

## What's in the sidebar

Two rows sit on their own at the top:

- **Dashboard** — The view described above. The badge counts everything waiting for your review.
- **Inbox** — Contact-form and channel messages. The badge counts unresolved threads.

Everything else is grouped by what it's for:

- **People** — **Members** (member accounts, roles, and credits, with volunteers and the hours
  queue as views) and **Bands** (all bands, their members, and moderation). Volunteer shifts
  are **Planning → Volunteering**, whose badge counts shifts in the next week short of bookings
  and shifts that ended. Every task,
  in a project or not, is on **Planning → Tasks**, with Projects, Recurring work, Productions
  and Templates as views beside the table. A task can wait on others: set it on the task's page
  under **Waits on**. It shows as **Blocked** on the table until they are done, and the project's
  **Task graph** draws the order.
- **Space** — **Reservations** (practice-space bookings and payments), with **Recurring** standing
  series and **Closures** blackout dates beneath it; and **Equipment** (the inventory), with
  **Loans** beneath it.
- **Programs** — **Events**: creating and running events, ticketing, and check-in.
- **Moderation** — **Content Flags** and **Suggestions**. What waits on you in either is on the
  dashboard.
- **Outreach** — **Campaigns**, **Audiences**, and **Help articles**.
- **Money** — **Payments** and **Credits**.
- **System** — **Settings**.

Click a group's heading to collapse it, and the sidebar remembers your choice next time. Opening a
page inside a group you collapsed re-opens that group, so you can always see where you are.

Most lists support search and filtering; click a row to open its detail page.
