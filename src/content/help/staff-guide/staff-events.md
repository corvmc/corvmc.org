## Managing events

**Staff → Calendar** lists every event by date with status, tags, and space-reservation
info, drafts included. Create a new one with **Create event**, or open an event to manage it.
Creating asks only for the title, kind, date and times; the event's page opens next, with its edit
form open, for everything else. An event with a production shows the production's status in the
**Production** column, which opens it.

**Staff → Productions** lists every production, from draft to closed. An event has no production
until someone opens one: **New production**, on **Staff → Productions**, attaches one to an event
already on the calendar, or makes the event and its production together. Each row opens its
event.

## On the event detail page

- **Listing form** — Title, description, date, start/end/doors times, where the show is, the
  bill and tickets, saved together. Under **Where**, **Hold from** and **Hold until** hold the
  practice space; leave both blank to hold nothing.
- **Poster, ticket sales and repeats** — the poster card, the ticket list and comps, and
  **Repeat event** to make it a series, all on the same page.
- **Publish / Unpublish** — Control whether the event is visible.
- **Cancel event** — Call off an event.
- **Rebook the hold** — If you change the time past the hold, press it to move the hold to the
  new times. Change **Hold from** or **Hold until** to move the hold without changing the
  event's times. Conflicts are flagged, and **Book anyway** overrides one deliberately.
  **Release hold** gives the room back when you save.

## The production console

A CMC show's **Manage production** button opens its console, which is back-of-house only: the
production record (status, event lead, host), deliverables, artifact requests and tech riders,
volunteer shifts, the run of show, and the budget and settlement. Anything the public sees is
edited on the event page; **View event** on the console takes you back there.

Booking names the show's **event lead** on the console. The lead can run the show from the console
and sees it on their member dashboard.

## Check-in

For ticketed events, open the **Check-in** tool from the event. Scan attendees'
ticket QR codes to admit them.
