## Campaigns

**Staff → Outreach → Campaigns** lists your email campaigns with their subject,
status (Draft, Scheduled, Sent), audiences, recipient count, and date.

## Creating a campaign

1. Choose **New Campaign**.
2. Write the **subject** and the **body** (markdown with a live preview).
3. Select one or more [audiences](/member/help/staff-audiences) to send to.
4. To email a show's ticket holders and RSVPs, pick the show under **About a show** and tick
   **Event audience**. **Email ticket holders** on the show's event page does both for you, and
   lists the campaigns already about that show.
5. Check the preview, then choose **Send test** to email a copy to your own address. Its
   unsubscribe link does nothing.
6. **Send now** or **schedule** it for a later time.

A show that is members-only or for one group gets a warning when an audience can reach people who
cannot see it. You can still send; the send is recorded in the event's history.

## Editing and viewing

- Drafts open in the editor so you can keep working on them.
- Sent campaigns open read-only. **Recipients** counts each send by status: sent, delivered, failed,
  bounced or complained. Staff who manage audiences also see each address, its audience, and why a
  send failed.

## Tips

- Always send a test to check formatting and links before a real send.
- Double-check the selected audiences and recipient count before sending.
