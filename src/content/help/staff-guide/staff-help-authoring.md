## Authoring help

**Staff → Content → Help articles** lists every help article, published or not. Create a new
one with **New article**, or edit an existing article.

## Creating an article

Fill in:

- **Title** and an optional **slug** (auto-generated from the title if left blank).
- **Category** and an optional **summary** for listings.
- **Content** in the markdown editor.
- **Audience** — Anyone, Members, Sustaining members or Staff — controls who can see it. A
  staff article also takes **Capabilities**; left empty, every position holder sees it.
- **Published** — tick it to make the article visible.

## Static vs. dynamic articles

- **Dynamic** articles are created and edited here in the web UI.
- **Static** articles are synced from markdown files in the codebase
  (`src/content/help/`). When you open one, a banner warns that **edits here will be
  overwritten on the next sync** — change the source file instead.

## Tips

- Set the right audience so staff-only guidance stays out of members' view.
- Keep an article focused on one task; link to related articles rather than
  cramming everything in.
