## What membership includes

Sustaining membership supports the collective and unlocks benefits such as:

- **[Practice credits](/member/help/practice-credits)** each month — an hour
  for every $5 you contribute.
- **Recurring reservations** for a standing practice schedule.
- **Gear library** perks: free accessory rentals, monthly equipment credits, and
  better rates on bigger gear.
- **Events and community** — first dibs on workshops and member-only events.
- Supporting the space so it stays open and affordable for everyone. Event
  tickets are on a sliding scale for everyone.

## The sliding scale

Contributions are on a sliding scale — choose an amount that fits your means.
Every amount gets the same benefits; the amount sets how many practice hours you
get each month.

## How credits are allocated

Your monthly **credit allocation** refreshes each billing cycle and is tracked
as a running balance. Your hours left are on the **Practice credits** card of
your **Dashboard**, and the **Credits** card on your **Membership** page shows
your monthly allocation.
