## My loans

Open **Equipment → My loans** to see everything you've requested or borrowed.
**Current** lists loans still in progress; **Past** lists the ones that are
finished.

Each loan shows its status:

- **Requested** — Submitted, waiting on staff approval.
- **Scheduled** — Approved, with a pickup date set.
- **Checked out** — With you now.
- **Returned** — Checked back in.
- **Cancelled** — Called off before pickup.

Current loans also show the pickup date, the due date and the cost; past ones
show when they were returned and what was charged. You can **Cancel** a loan
until it is checked out to you.

## Returning gear

Bring the item back by its return date and staff will check it in. Returning on
time keeps the library stocked for other members.
