## Electronic Press Kit (EPK)

Every band has a press kit for the information venues, bookers, and press need.
Owners and admins open it from **Press kit** in the band's sidebar. It is free,
whether or not you have a band site, and what you enter feeds your page's Press,
Achievements and Contact blocks.

## What to fill in

- **Press quotes** — Review pull-quotes, with the **Publication** and **Date**.
- **Highlights** — Awards, notable supports, festivals, milestones.
- **Video** — Live clips, on Premium.
- **Contacts** — Separate **Booking**, **Management** and **Press** contacts.
  These go only in the press kit you download, never on your public page.
- **Press photos** — One on the free plan; Premium lifts the limit.

What you need on stage is a separate page: the band's **Tech rider**.

## Saving and sending

Choose **Save press kit** to save it. **Download press kit (.zip)** gives you the
kit to send. Keep it current — it's often the first thing a booker looks at.
