## Arranging your page

Your page arrives already built. Every block is on it from the start, so the
**Page editor** is about arranging what's there rather than assembling it:

1. **Reordering** — Drag a block by its handle, or use its up and down arrows.
2. **Configuring each block** — The settings button in a block's control strip
   opens its properties (text, items, links, and so on).
3. **Hiding** — The eye button stops publishing any block you have nothing for. A hidden block keeps
   everything you put in it, so you can show it again later.

A block with nothing to show doesn't appear on your live page even when it's
visible — an empty Merch shelf or a Press section with no quotes yet simply
isn't rendered. Hiding is for blocks you have content for but don't want
published.

## Preview as you go

The editor shows your page as it will look. **View site →** opens your live page
in a new tab; it picks up your changes once you choose **Save changes**.
