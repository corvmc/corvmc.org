## Deleting your account

If you no longer want a CorvMC account, you can delete it from **Account**.

1. Select your picture at the top right, then **Account**.
2. In the **Security** section, choose **Delete account**.
3. Enter your **Password** and choose **Delete my account**.

Staff accounts cannot be deleted this way — contact an admin.

## Before you delete

- **This can't be undone.** Your profile, directory listing, and personal data are
  removed.
- **Your reservations and membership end with it.** Deleting cancels all your
  current and future reservations and ends a sustaining membership. To keep your
  account but stop paying, [cancel the membership](/member/help/manage-billing)
  instead.

If you only want to stop receiving messages, you don't need to delete your
account — adjust your
[notification preferences](/member/help/notification-preferences) and
[email subscriptions](/member/help/email-subscriptions) instead.
