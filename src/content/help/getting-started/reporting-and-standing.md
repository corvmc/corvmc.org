## Reporting

Most things members write are reportable from the thing itself — a member or
band profile, an event listing on the gig guide, a suggestion, or a message
someone sent you. Look for the report action on the page.

Tell us what is wrong rather than that something is wrong. "The date is wrong"
is a correction; "this is not their band" is a different problem with a
different answer.

Two reports behave differently from the rest, because reporting them does
something immediately:

- **A suggestion** comes off the board while staff look at it.
- **A private conversation** becomes readable by staff. That is the only way we
  can ever read one, and it only exposes the conversation you were in.

You are not told what staff decided about someone else. Reporting is not a
notification service.

## If a report about you is upheld

Two separate things can happen, and they are decided separately.

**The post.** It may come down or be turned down. For an event listing that is
not the end of it — a turned-down listing is editable and you can put it back up
once it is fixed.

**Your standing.** Staff may put your future posts through review first. This is
scoped to where it happened: a report upheld about a gig listing affects your
gig listings. It does not touch your suggestions, and it does not touch your
messages. Being restricted in one place is not being restricted everywhere.

What review looks like depends on the area:

| Area               | Restricted means                                                  |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Event listings** | Your listings are checked before they go on the calendar          |
| **Suggestions**    | Your suggestions are checked before they appear on the board      |
| **Messages**       | You can reply to conversations you are in, but not start new ones |

Messaging is the one that can also be switched off entirely.

You are told when this happens, and you are told the reason staff recorded. You
are not left to work it out from a button that stopped working.

## Getting it lifted

Staff can restore your standing, and doing so is a normal part of the process
rather than an exception — most restrictions are meant to be temporary.

Ask through [Messages](/member/help/messages-overview). If your messaging is the
thing that was restricted, reply-only still lets you answer any conversation
staff open with you, and the restriction notice itself is a conversation you can
reply to.

A restriction that is lifted still reads differently from one that never
happened — we keep the record of both, so "why was I in review?" stays
answerable afterwards.
