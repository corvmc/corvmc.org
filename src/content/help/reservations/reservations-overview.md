## Practice space

CorvMC's rehearsal rooms are bookable by the hour. Open **Practice space** from the
sidebar to see your bookings and make new ones with **Reserve practice space**.

## The Practice space page

- **Rate, Hours, Length and Notice** — The current booking terms. **Booking
  policy** opens the rest.
- Credits — How many hours of practice credits you have left and when they reset.
- **My reservations** — **Upcoming** and **All** tabs switch between your upcoming
  reservations and your full history. Each card shows the date, time, duration,
  and status.
- **My recurring reservations** — Your standing weekly, every-other-week or monthly
  bookings. Without a sustaining membership this is **Recurring reservations**,
  with a link to learn more.

## Good to know

- Sustaining members get a monthly allotment of **practice credits**; see
  [practice credits](/member/help/practice-credits).
- Some slots are **waitlisted** and need confirming once a spot opens — see
  [confirm a waitlisted reservation](/member/help/confirm-waitlisted).
- The space may be closed on certain dates for holidays or maintenance; those
  times won't be bookable.
