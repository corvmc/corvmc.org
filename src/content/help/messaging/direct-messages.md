## Why this exists

The directory is only useful if you can act on it. Someone who reads a bassist's
profile should be able to reach that bassist without needing an email address or
an Instagram handle — and the bassist should not have to publish one to be
reachable.

## Sending a first message

From **Messages**, press **Message a Member** and search for them by name. You
can reach anyone you can already see in the directory.

Your first message goes as a **request**. It is one message — you cannot send
again until they accept — and it stays out of their notifications until they do.

You will not be told whether they read it, declined it, have messages switched
off, or have blocked you. All four look identical from your side, on purpose: a
decline that is visible is a decline people hesitate to make, and the whole
point of consent is that saying no should cost nothing.

Two limits worth knowing: you can have a handful of unanswered requests out at
once, and there is a daily cap on starting new conversations. Both exist to make
bulk messaging impractical, and neither is something an ordinary conversation
runs into.

## When someone messages you

A request shows on the Messages page with a **Request** badge. You have three
choices:

- **Accept** — it becomes a normal conversation and you can both write freely.
- **Decline** — the conversation closes and that person cannot start another.
  They are not told.
- **Report** — the same as declining, and it also sends the conversation to
  staff to look at. Use it when the message was not just unwanted but wrong.

Reporting is the only thing that lets staff read a private conversation, and it
only ever exposes the thread you were actually in.

## Blocking

Inside any open conversation you can block the other person. Blocking works both
ways from a single action — neither of you can start something new — and it
takes effect on sending, replying, and accepting.

It deliberately does **not** hide the conversation from you. If you blocked
someone because of what they said, you still need to be able to read it to
report it.

Manage everyone you have blocked from **Account** → the messaging section, where
you can also unblock. Unblocking is immediate and leaves no record.

## Turning messages off

Also in **Account**: a switch that stops other members starting conversations
with you at all. Existing conversations keep working, and staff can always reach
you — this is about unsolicited messages from members, not about being
contactable.

Anyone who tries while it is off sees exactly what they would see if you had
simply not replied.

## If staff have restricted your messaging

Two things staff can do, both of which say so plainly when you try:

- **Reply-only** — you can answer conversations you are already in, but not start
  new ones.
- **Off** — you cannot send at all.

Either way you are shown the reason staff recorded. See
[Reporting something, and what happens next](/member/help/reporting-and-standing).
