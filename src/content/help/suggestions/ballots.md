## Two kinds of ballot

**Ballots** in the member panel lists every vote you are part of, and every result that has been
certified.

- A **committee ballot** is a vote of one committee, such as the board. Everyone on the committee's
  roster when the ballot opens can vote. The vote is **recorded**: once voting closes, who voted which
  way is shown beside the result, the way minutes would show it. You can change your vote until
  voting closes.
- A **member-wide ballot** is a vote of the whole membership. The vote is **secret**. You can vote
  once and cannot change it afterwards.

## Who can vote on a member-wide ballot

You can vote if all of these were true on the day the ballot opened:

- your account was active, and not deactivated or banned;
- you had been a member for at least 60 days;
- you had completed orientation, or staff had waived it.

The roll is fixed when the ballot opens. Joining, reaching 60 days or finishing orientation later does
not add you to that ballot. If you think you were left off by mistake, ask staff. They can add you,
and they record why.

Nobody can vote on your behalf. There are no proxies.

## What "secret" means here

When you vote on a member-wide ballot, the app records two separate things:

- that **you voted**, so you cannot vote twice;
- **one more vote** for the choice you picked.

Nothing links the two, and neither has a time on it. Staff can see who voted, but not what anyone
chose. The ballot is secret from the app and from anyone using it. It is not a cryptographic
election, and anyone with access to the hosting account could in principle compare database
backups.

## Results

Nobody sees a running tally, including staff. Once voting closes, the electors can see the result.
The certifier named on the ballot then **certifies** it, which fixes it as the official result and
tells every member. A certified result is open to every member.

## Running a committee ballot

If you are an owner or admin of a committee, **New committee ballot** on the Ballots page starts a
draft. It asks for:

- the question;
- the choices, one per line;
- the day voting closes (at 23:59 that day);
- who certifies the result.

Edit the draft until it is right, then press **Open voting**. The roll is fixed at that moment and
everyone on it is told.
