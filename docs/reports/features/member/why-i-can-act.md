# Why I can act

Status: ✅ /member/volunteer/profile

The volunteer profile lists the member's own staff positions, committee seats (with the committee positions they hold) and current shift roles, each with the capabilities it gives, and shows nothing when they hold none (#2994). A task's page says why the member may act on it: assigned, holds the position it waits on, a seat on its committee, or a grant. Only the member's own access is shown; another person's is staff-only (`user.read`).
