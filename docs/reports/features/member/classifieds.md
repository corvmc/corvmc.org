# Classifieds

Status: ✅ /member/classifieds

Members-only wanted/offered posts (musicians, jams, services, gear, other) with instrument, genre and skill tags, optionally posted as a band the author runs. A post lasts 30 days, can be renewed or closed by its author, and expiry is derived at read time rather than stored. Answered through a DM request (the author's profile when `directMessages` is off). A report withholds the post; an upheld one hides it with a staff note and restricts the author's `classified` standing, and an edit sends it back for review. Nothing is deleted. Gear posts are for sale, wanted or trade (trade is gear-only), contacted by DM with no payments in the app, and carry a disclaimer that CMC is not party to the sale (#1488). See `docs/development/business-workflows.md` §25
