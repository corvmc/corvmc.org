# Committee positions

Status: ✅ /member/groups/[slug]

A committee's chair names its standing positions and chooses who holds each, from the Positions card on the Roster tab (#2988); staff with `group.manage` can as well. A position belongs to one committee, may have several holders or none, and goes with the holder's roster row when they leave. The roster's Position column, the member's directory profile and the Bands tab of the staff member record list the positions a person holds. Band and club seat titles stay free text.
