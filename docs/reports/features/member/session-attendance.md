# Session attendance

Status: ✅ /member/groups/[slug]

A club or committee lead marks who came to a past session from the Sessions tab (#2718). The list is the active roster plus anyone who RSVPed, so a walk-in from the roster needs no RSVP. Marks are `event_attendance` rows, apart from `event_rsvp`; the Roster tab shows each member's count of sessions attended.
