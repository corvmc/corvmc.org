# Volunteer hours

Status: ✅ /member/volunteer

One-time onboarding (name, phone, 18-or-older) gates the module; under-18 answers are blocked pending staff approval. Then: pick roles grouped at-shows/away-from-shows/committees plus an availability note, sign up for and drop shifts, one row per shift so a task's several shifts each show (interest-first ordering, clearance gates say what's missing), pre-filled hour logs from completed shifts (an unconfirmed claim completes as worked too), log/edit/withdraw hours (approved on entry unless an exception holds it, and editable until a staffer approves it), day-after feedback survey. A shift staffing a show names it on the card. The 2026 redesign made the dashboard a next-action stack beside the claim board: work owing hours leads (shifts worked, and tasks marked done, logged against the task), then **Assigned tasks** with Mark done, then the shifts you are on with a two-step **Signed up → Booked** rail, then three summary rows that link out. Interests and the hour history became their own screens — a log filed in March is not a next action, and a returned one was buried under everything approved; it is now tinted, carries the staff reason in full and offers `Fix it`. The board filters to the roles you picked, with `Show all` one press away. Profile stays a header modal, since nothing else edits the volunteer record
