# Back door check-in

Status: ✅ /door

#2120. The QR code on the back door opens `/door` (signed out: sign-in, then back). The booker or any active member of the booked band gets the booking open from 30 minutes before its start: **Check in** sends `st.lock` `unlock` after a health read (nothing is queued for an offline lock — U-tec documents no expiry, #2157), polls `lockState` for the ack's deferral, and follows an unconfirmed unlock with a `lock`. A timeout or offline lock reveals the booking's pool code, which was claimed on load and sent with the page, so it still shows with no signal; an offline reveal is reported once the phone reconnects. **Open door** for re-entry; **Check out** shows the leaving checklist (`venue.checkoutTasks`, Practice space settings → The Room) and locks. Every attempt is a `door_access` row; the staff booking page shows check-in, check-out, attempts and the code, and **Read out a code** claims one for a phone call. Booking pages link to `/door` in place of a code. Help: `check-in-at-the-door`
