# Band chat

Status: ✅ /band/[slug]/messages

**One page, two inboxes.** The band's own rooms (`group`, every active member) and its booking enquiries (`band`, owner and admin) are two lists in one `ListDetailShell` list pane, reached by one Messages nav row — they were two rows, so a member had to know which one a message arrived on. The lists stay separate because their readers do; `/band/[slug]/chat` 308s to `/band/[slug]/messages`. **Chat is topics, not one room** (#1301): a topic is an `inbox_thread` with a `subject`, a null subject being General, so the threads that already existed became General with no migration. General pins to the top of the list and the rest sort by most recent; the dot is per topic off `inbox_group_read`, which was already keyed that way, and an empty topic is never unread. Any active member opens one — a room the whole group reads is not an admin's to ration — and the name is unique per group, case-insensitively, with `General` reserved. Clubs and committees get the same topics at `/member/groups/[slug]/chat`. Booking enquiries from the public form land as conversations the act reads and answers, instead of one email nobody has a record of. A fourth inbox channel (`band`) plus the nullable owner column `docs/architecture/domain-model.md` argued for — `inbox_thread.group_id`, null meaning CMC. Ownership is resolved **live** from the roster, not snapshotted, so a new admin inherits the back catalogue and someone who leaves loses it; that settles the snapshot-vs-live question the groups design left open, and is why a band thread carries **zero `inbox_participant` rows** (one would surface a booking negotiation in `/member/messages`) with the per-reader cursor in `inbox_group_read` instead. Two-way over the existing signed per-thread Reply-To: the act answers on the site, the booker gets email from "<Band> via CorvMC" on the `band-reply` template, and their answer routes back into the thread — **neither side ever sees the other's address**. Owner/admin, the Press Kit line; the mutations resolve the band from the _thread_ rather than a slug, so a caller cannot name their own band and someone else's conversation. Staff see nothing: `staffVisibleThread` excludes `band` and `group` beside `direct` (all three in one `PRIVATE_CHANNELS` list, because writing it out twice is how `group` was missed — #1296), `getThread()` refuses all three, and the channel is absent from Staff Settings and the queue filter — a report is the only way in. One email survives, unchanged: a press-kit booking address belonging to no active owner or admin, i.e. an off-platform manager. See `docs/decisions/0041-band-enquiries-are-threads-the-band-owns.md`
