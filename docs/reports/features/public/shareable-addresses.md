# Shareable addresses

Status: ✅ /m/[memberNumber] + group subdomains

The free tier, named. Every member has `corvmc.org/m/{memberNumber}` and every group has `{slug}.corvmc.org` — the thing link-in-bio services charge for, which this app always gave away and never called anything. `user.memberNumber` existed but nothing assigned it: `assignMemberNumber` (`src/lib/server/user/member-number-service.ts`) now issues one in the `user.create.after` hook, non-fatally and retrying once past a unique-index collision, and `scripts/backfill-member-numbers.ts` numbers accounts that predate it (run by hand, not from a migration). `/m/[memberNumber]` is a server-side 302 modelled on `/a/[tag]`, routed by viewer through `entityHref`. `canonicalAddress` (`src/lib/utils/canonical-address.ts`) is the single answer to "what is X's address"; `ShareButton` takes it as `url` so a profile shares the address rather than the path it is being viewed at, and `AddressCard` shows it with a `qrcode-svg` QR on `/member/profile` and the band dashboard. External acts get none, per `docs/decisions/0022-external-acts-are-staff-records-with-private-contacts.md`. Also fixes clubs and committees: their subdomains resolved but redirected to the band directory, which requires kind `band` and 404s — `groupPublicPath` routes them to `/groups/{slug}`.
