# Ticket purchase

Status: ✅ Full flow

A sliding scale and a split bar. `event.ticketPrice` is the **suggested** price and `ticketPriceFloorCents` is the bottom of the scale — 0 by default, which runs it all the way to free and makes NOTAFLOF operational online for the first time (at $0 the purchase skips Stripe entirely and mints valid tickets outright, capped per email so a free show can't be emptied by one person). A two-way `SplitBar` then divides what they paid between the acts on the bill and the collective, opening at a suggested 70/30 of what is **divisible** — card processing is a locked third slice off the top — with a cover-fees checkbox and a reset. The acts' 70% is a guarantee against the **suggested** price rather than against what was handed over, so paying under it shorts the collective and never the band, while everything above it stays the buyer's to direct: on a $10 show a $20 buyer may send the collective up to $12.12, not $5.12. Paying above the suggestion is still the `ticket_contribution` line item, so Stripe reporting and the receipt breakdown are unchanged. **Recorded, not routed:** every dollar lands in CMC's Stripe account and `ticket.actsCents` / `collectiveCents` / `feeCoveredCents` are what staff settle from — an act is paid the way a contractor is, so no touring band needs a Connect account to get paid. The 50% sustaining-member ticket discount is **removed**: half off a pay-what-you-can ticket is a discount off nothing. Staff see the totals as sold on the production page, plus a free/paid split. See `docs/decisions/0008-ticket-price-is-a-sliding-scale.md`
