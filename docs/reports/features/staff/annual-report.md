# Annual report

Status: ✅ /staff/reports/annual

One page and one CSV answering what the collective earned, spent, was given and held for others over a date range, beside donated time, events held, practice-room use and membership. Every figure comes from the module's own report service — `totalsByKindAndCategory` over `financial_entry`, `getVolunteerTotals`, `getContributedValue`, `getEventTotals`, `getRoomUseTotals`, `getCommunityStats` — so a number here cannot drift from the page it came from. The money section is grouped by `kind` rather than summed: in-kind and pass-through are shown and deliberately excluded from net, because a naive total claims income the collective never had. **Coverage is stated rather than assumed**: the ledger begins where the app's payment history does, and a range reaching further back says so instead of rendering a confident under-count. Events are counts with no attendance figure, and room hours include comped and credit-covered time, so they do not track the revenue line. Behind `finance.read`; the CSV is long-format (`section, line, value, unit`) with the caveats written into the file. See `docs/decisions/0040-reports-are-module-owned.md`
