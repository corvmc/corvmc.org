# Music

Status: ✅ /staff/music

Every band's releases in one table, filtered by status; a row opens the release's panel with withhold/restore (a takedown is `withheld`, not `draft`, so a band cannot simply republish it) and per-release radio veto, kept distinct from the band's own opt-in. Also the radio readiness panel — eligible **tracks** and distinct **bands**, not releases, because a rotation of forty tracks by two bands sounds like two bands — and each release's recent sales with the refund. Sales totals and the realised take rate, the number the refusable-cut decision is judged on, are the Music report at `/staff/reports/music` (#2766). Not behind a flag, on purpose
