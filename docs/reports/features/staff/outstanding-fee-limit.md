# Outstanding fee limit

Status: ✅ Setting

A member's own booking checks their outstanding fees (#2748): sessions they booked that ended still payment due (`confirmedUnpaidSql`) and started on or after Practice space settings → Outstanding fees → **Count from**. One fee refuses the booking in `bookAndPayReservation`, `bookBandReservation` and `bookInstructorReservation`; the booking button opens Payment due with Message staff instead, and the `door_code_ready` reminder skips the member until it is paid (#2859). Member pages show no door code or break-glass code for the member's bookings while a fee is owed, bandmates' included, with the payment-due notice in its place; staff pages keep it (#2874). A band booking checks the booking member's own fees. Staff creating a booking on a member's behalf see the fees and are never refused. Empty **Count from** turns it off; older payment-due rows stay on the staff list and never count. Each fee is emailed to the booker 1, 7 and 21 days after the session, once per stage, with every attempt in the audit log (#2876, `payment-reminder.ts`). `src/lib/server/reservation/outstanding-fees.ts`.
