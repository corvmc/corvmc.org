# Sponsors

Status: ✅ /staff/funding

Businesses that sponsor the collective (an `organization` holding `sponsor`) and each term of support (`sponsorship`: title, tier, status pitched → active → ended or declined, amount, term dates). Each sponsorship is a project of kind `sponsorship`, listed on the Funding page with its next task (#2622); a term past its end reads as lapsed on the sponsor's page until it is ended or renewed. Guarded by `sponsor.read` / `sponsor.manage`, which a committee's grant list can also carry (seeded on Development, #1578); the treasurer reads. The placement form's show picker is `searchEventsForPlacement`, guarded by `sponsor.manage`, so a committee member who cannot read the staff event list can still place a sponsorship (#1618). Reminds grant and sponsor managers 14 and 3 days before each deadline, through the reminder registry (#1477), and lists the next 30 days on the staff dashboard. A sponsorship can be placed on a show (`sponsor_placement`, #583), which credits the sponsor on the public event page and/or in blasts about that show. Only active or ended terms are credited, disclosed as "Sponsored by". The sponsor's logo is a `media` attachment. See business-workflows §21
