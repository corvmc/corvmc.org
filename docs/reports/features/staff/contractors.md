# Contractors

Status: ✅ /staff/contacts?role=contractor

Paid outside work, listed as the Contacts contractor view and shown on each contractor's Contacts page since #2764; `/staff/contractors` redirects there. — the other of the two places a broken thing gets fixed. a contractor is an organization holding the `contractor` role with `contractor_detail` (trade, licence, insurance expiry), its people on Contacts and `contractor_job` — one contractor's engagement on a work order (asked → booked → completed, or declined / no-show / cancelled) — covering both a unit that goes out to an instrument tech and building service that owns no inventory row at all. Booking a repair takes the unit out of service and completing it brings it back, both through `setAssetStatus` so the stock ledger and the job agree; cancelling or a no-show deliberately leaves it out, since calling off the tech does not mend the amp. Late is derived from `expectedBackAt`, never stored. The Schedule's Contractor filter (`/staff/volunteer/schedule?kind=contractor`) lists the work orders with an engagement, under Open, Late, Awaiting invoice and All; `/staff/contractors/jobs` redirects there. A contractor is engaged from the work order's page. An engagement has no page of its own: its card, with Book, Complete, Decline, No-show, Cancel and Invoice, sits on its work order's page, and `/staff/contractors/jobs/[id]` redirects there. Cost sits on the job rather than on `acquisition` — a labor invoice is not stock arriving — and surfaces as a Services block on `/staff/reports/inventory-spend`. Service history shows on the unit's own page. Invoices are a reference string, not an upload, while the R2 bucket is public. See `docs/decisions/0051-contractor-work-has-its-own-tables.md`
