# Productions

Status: 🔧 Partial

The `production` record is built: 1:1 with `event_listing` (cascade), opened and worked on in the event page's **Production** tab (`/staff/events/[id]?tab=production`, #3270; the old `/production` address redirects there), with the event lead and shifts in the page's Crew sidebar. **The event lead is a volunteer role (#3274)**: an "Event lead" task with no shift, made with the production and assigned from the Crew box by `volunteer.manageRoster`. Its assignee holds `production.run` on the show's project and `event.manageGuests` on its event from assignment until 7 days after the show; the old `producer_user_id` grant still works beside it until a later change retires it. Carries an optional **acts wanted** target, so a bill can say it is not finished — `/staff/productions` lists every production at every status and filters to shows short of acts (#3112). Carries the producer, load-in → soundcheck → first set → curfew → load-out, and three notes fields; the status machine is `draft → offered → confirmed → completed → settled → closed` with `cancelled` off any pre-completed state, moved by atomic conditional update. `settled` and `closed` exist in the vocabulary and in `StatusBadge` but have no button — they are Phase 5/6. Cancelling a listing pulls its pre-completed production back with it. Guarded as `event.read` / `event.manage`, on the venue precedent: no `production.*` resource, and no `production_lead` position. **No new route** — the productions index stays the CMC event list, now the Productions view at `/staff/events?view=productions` and absorbed the columns instead (production status, venue, lineup summary, plus venue and date-range filters, all inside its one query). **Run of show** is built: `production_slot`, the timing child of `event_band` 1:1, keyed on `event_band_id` set-null so a dropped credit leaves the slot intact and on `production_id` so it is never orphaned. It carries set length, changeover, a manual soundcheck, tech/backline/hospitality notes and a per-show contact picked from the act's people (`contact_id`); `sort_order` is a fractional `real` with **no** unique constraint, because SQLite enforces one per-row as an UPDATE walks. Set times are derived by the pure `computeSetTimes` from `production.first_set_at`, rewritten on every mutation that can move one — no override field, no lock flag, no recalculate button — and validation is warnings computed on read (past curfew, before doors, a set over four hours, a soundcheck after the downbeat) rather than errors, because real shows run late. The Run of show tab adds, reorders by adjacent swap, edits and removes sets, and builds the whole running order from the bill in one click. **Artifact requests** close the advance's other half: `artifact_request` is one live ask per (event, act, artifact) — tech rider, press kit or poster art — with a due date, surfaced as an **Asked for** panel on the Advance tab that asks, cancels, and shows waiting / overdue / in. Arrival is **derived from the artifact itself** rather than stored, so a rider filled in unprompted already counts and nothing is ticked off by hand; asking again is a reminder, not a second row. A request is against the act's `directory_entry`, which is why an artist commissioned for poster art can receive one without ever being on the bill. **An act with no CMC account can now answer the rider ask**: `rider` is keyed on `group_id`, so the structured summary is permanently empty for one and the request read as outstanding forever (#863). `/act/{token}` takes a rider **file** — PDF or a photo of a hand-drawn plot — through `/api/acts/[token]/rider` into the `rider` slot of `media_attachment`, which was already polymorphic, and fulfilment reads either. Hospitality remains a one-way field (`production_slot.hospitality_notes`) that acts cannot read. **The deal shape** is built, per act rather than per show: `{ guarantee_cents, percentage_bps, versus, against_net, contributed }` on `production_slot` — the same sparsity argument that put the table there, since `event_band` is what the public gig guide and every band page join. `describeTerms` in `#lib/production/terms.js` renders all five shapes (donated, flat fee, pure split, guarantee versus a percentage, guarantee plus one) and is shared by the console and the band's own page so the two cannot phrase one deal two ways. Editing is a **separate form** from the timing one on the Run of show tab, which is the seam a settlement capability would guard. The act-facing half is a read-only **Bookings** panel on `/band/{slug}/events`, showing that band's own call times and terms from `offered` onward and never another act's or a whole-show total; an outside act sees the same, plus its show contact and what it was paid, on `/act/{token}` (#2721) — visibility, not immutability, is what protects a deal from settling at the wrong number. Not built: close-out (Phase 6). The spec's 70/30 pool, `bandSplitPercent`, `production.venueId`, `productions` feature flag and `/staff/productions/[id]` are all superseded — see `docs/development/business-workflows.md#14-producing-a-show-the-production-record` **A payout is now recorded**: `production_slot.paidCents` says what an act was actually handed — staff's number, not the worksheet's, since a settlement is a conversation at the end of the night — and the ledger gets it as a negative `pass_through` against the show's pool, so `poolBalanceCents` walks to zero and "what does this show still owe" stays answerable. A payout larger than the pool splits: the covered part is pass-through, the excess is `spent` under `act_guarantee`, because a guarantee on a soft night is the collective's own money rather than somebody else's passing through. Guarded by `finance.refund` rather than the console's `event.manage` — a producer runs the show, a treasurer says what was paid. **What the night cost is recorded too**: `production_expense` had a service since #839 and no writer, so the worksheet's Expenses figure was structurally zero and an `against_net` deal divided against nothing. The Settlement tab now adds, lists and removes cost lines — label, category, amount, who was paid, and a `deductible` flag, since a cost the collective carries whatever happens is real spend but is not the act's to share. `expensesCents` reads that cost sheet rather than the ledger's `spent` rows, which also hold the guarantee top-ups `netCents` already subtracts. Guarded `event.manage`, not `finance.refund`: booking the engineer is the producer's own work and nothing leaves the till when the line is written. **The advance checks riders against stock** (#2724): the Advance tab's Requested gear card lists each item an act asks CMC to supply, matched to the inventory catalog by a staff link (`rider_element.inventory_item_id`, `production.run`) or else suggested by category name, with what no category serves listed as not in stock and a shortfall when one act asks for more than is free. **Lineup and run of show (#3272):** The Production tab is the only place a CMC show's acts are edited; the event page's Bill card lists them read-only with an **Edit lineup** link. **Lineup** holds one row per act: play order (▲/▼), set length, remove, the rider link or **Ask for rider**, and the deal (**Set deal**, `production.book`). The header reads "N of M booked", where M is the editable **bands wanted**. **Add act** searches CMC bands and outside acts; with no match it offers **Create act** (saved as an outside act with hometown and contact email) or a plain name that is not saved. `addLineupAct` / `removeLineupAct` (`production.book`) go through `lineup-service.ts`, which rewrites the bill with `setEventLineup` and adds or removes the set in the same call, so a credit and its set cannot drift apart; removing a linked band's credit withdraws its claim on the show, and a set already paid stays, detached from the credit. **Run of show** is one list of `[time] · label · Done`: load-in, soundcheck, doors (the listing's, read-only), the sets, curfew, load-out. A time is entered as a clock time and put on the show date, or after midnight when before 06:00 (`showClockTime`); the first set's time is editable and later sets follow from play order, set lengths and one **changeover** per show (`production.changeover_minutes`, 15 by default, moving every set after the first). **Done** records the actual time: `production.*_done_at` for the fixed rows, `production_slot.actual_start_at` for a set. Billing and hospitality notes are gone from the tab; a migration copies their text into internal notes and the columns stay for now. Not built here: ticket sales on the tab (the Listing tab's ticketing card still carries them), settlement till counts and costs (#3273). **Guests per act (#3274):** each lineup row shows a guest count and the names under it, for staff and the event lead (`event.manageGuests`). A guest is a free one-seat ticket with `ticket.event_band_id` set (`addActGuest`, `guest-service.ts`), issued through `issueFreeTickets` so the holder gets the ordinary ticket email and the audit row is `event.tickets_comped`. Guests count against the sale's capacity and an act on a cancelled show takes none. `removeActGuest` cancels a guest. Dropping an act from the bill cancels its `pending` and `valid` guests inside `setEventLineup`'s write, whichever path dropped it, and sends no email; a checked-in guest stays. The Ticket sales comp button is unchanged. Removing the unused billing and hospitality columns is a later migration. **Till counts (#3273, in progress):** the door cash lives in `production_till_count`, one row per handoff (`kind = 'handoff'`, with who counted and when) and at most one staff count (`kind = 'staff'`, a partial unique index). The settlement's cash is the staff count when there is one, else the handoffs' sum, else nobody counted. `till-count-service.ts` writes (`recordHandoff`, `removeHandoff`, `recordStaffCount`, which replaces the staff row and sets the headcount and split in one batch) and `till-summary.ts` reads. One person making the staff count and every handoff warns as `oneCounter`; it never refuses. `Settlement.settled` is true when every act has a payout recorded. The Settlement tab's door card lists the handoffs and takes the staff count through `recordStaffCount` (`production.run`); the volunteer count form is on the check-in screens; removing the console section is a later phase. **Settle dialog (#3273):** a CMC show's header button is **Settle event** from its start time; it opens `SettleDialog.svelte` over the page's own query (`getStaffEventPage` returns `settle`: the settlement, `canCount` and `canPayout`, only to a reader of the production console). The dialog shows the net after costs, takes the staff count, and lists each unpaid act with an editable payout and a **Donated set** box; **Record payouts** (`recordPayouts`, `finance.refund`) records them all. Once every act is paid the header shows Settled and the button reads **View settlement**, the same figures read-only with a per-act **Undo payout** for `finance.refund`. Removing a handoff count is audited (`production.till_count_removed`). The six `door_cash_*` columns on `production` are backfilled into the table and no longer read or written; dropping them rebuilds `production`.
