# Productions

Status: 🔧 Partial

The `production` record is built: 1:1 with `event_listing` (cascade), opened by **Add production** on `/staff/events/[id]` and worked on in the console at `/staff/events/[id]/production`, which now carries a four-tab set (Overview / Advance / Run of show / Tickets) addressable by `?tab=`. Carries an optional **acts wanted** target, so a bill can say it is not finished — the productions index badges what is still owed and filters to shows short of acts. Carries the producer, load-in → soundcheck → first set → curfew → load-out, and three notes fields; the status machine is `draft → offered → confirmed → completed → settled → closed` with `cancelled` off any pre-completed state, moved by atomic conditional update. `settled` and `closed` exist in the vocabulary and in `StatusBadge` but have no button — they are Phase 5/6. Cancelling a listing pulls its pre-completed production back with it. Guarded as `event.read` / `event.manage`, on the venue precedent: no `production.*` resource, and no `production_lead` position. **No new route** — the productions index stays the CMC event list, now the Productions view at `/staff/events?view=productions` and absorbed the columns instead (production status, venue, lineup summary, plus venue and date-range filters, all inside its one query). **Run of show** is built: `production_slot`, the timing child of `event_band` 1:1, keyed on `event_band_id` set-null so a dropped credit leaves the slot intact and on `production_id` so it is never orphaned. It carries set length, changeover, a manual soundcheck, tech/backline/hospitality notes and a per-show contact picked from the act's people (`contact_id`); `sort_order` is a fractional `real` with **no** unique constraint, because SQLite enforces one per-row as an UPDATE walks. Set times are derived by the pure `computeSetTimes` from `production.first_set_at`, rewritten on every mutation that can move one — no override field, no lock flag, no recalculate button — and validation is warnings computed on read (past curfew, before doors, a set over four hours, a soundcheck after the downbeat) rather than errors, because real shows run late. The Run of show tab adds, reorders by adjacent swap, edits and removes sets, and builds the whole running order from the bill in one click. **Artifact requests** close the advance's other half: `artifact_request` is one live ask per (event, act, artifact) — tech rider, press kit or poster art — with a due date, surfaced as an **Asked for** panel on the Advance tab that asks, cancels, and shows waiting / overdue / in. Arrival is **derived from the artifact itself** rather than stored, so a rider filled in unprompted already counts and nothing is ticked off by hand; asking again is a reminder, not a second row. A request is against the act's `directory_entry`, which is why an artist commissioned for poster art can receive one without ever being on the bill. **An act with no CMC account can now answer the rider ask**: `rider` is keyed on `group_id`, so the structured summary is permanently empty for one and the request read as outstanding forever (#863). `/act/{token}` takes a rider **file** — PDF or a photo of a hand-drawn plot — through `/api/acts/[token]/rider` into the `rider` slot of `media_attachment`, which was already polymorphic, and fulfilment reads either. Hospitality remains a one-way field (`production_slot.hospitality_notes`) that acts cannot read. **The deal shape** is built, per act rather than per show: `{ guarantee_cents, percentage_bps, versus, against_net, contributed }` on `production_slot` — the same sparsity argument that put the table there, since `event_band` is what the public gig guide and every band page join. `describeTerms` in `#lib/production/terms.js` renders all five shapes (donated, flat fee, pure split, guarantee versus a percentage, guarantee plus one) and is shared by the console and the band's own page so the two cannot phrase one deal two ways. Editing is a **separate form** from the timing one on the Run of show tab, which is the seam a settlement capability would guard. The act-facing half is a read-only **Bookings** panel on `/band/{slug}/events`, showing that band's own call times and terms from `offered` onward and never another act's or a whole-show total; an outside act sees the same, plus its show contact and what it was paid, on `/act/{token}` (#2721) — visibility, not immutability, is what protects a deal from settling at the wrong number. Not built: close-out (Phase 6). The spec's 70/30 pool, `bandSplitPercent`, `production.venueId`, `productions` feature flag and `/staff/productions/[id]` are all superseded — see `docs/decisions/0003-a-production-is-a-child-of-its-listing.md` **A payout is now recorded**: `production_slot.paidCents` says what an act was actually handed — staff's number, not the worksheet's, since a settlement is a conversation at the end of the night — and the ledger gets it as a negative `pass_through` against the show's pool, so `poolBalanceCents` walks to zero and "what does this show still owe" stays answerable. A payout larger than the pool splits: the covered part is pass-through, the excess is `spent` under `act_guarantee`, because a guarantee on a soft night is the collective's own money rather than somebody else's passing through. Guarded by `finance.refund` rather than the console's `event.manage` — a producer runs the show, a treasurer says what was paid. **What the night cost is recorded too**: `production_expense` had a service since #839 and no writer, so the worksheet's Expenses figure was structurally zero and an `against_net` deal divided against nothing. The Settlement tab now adds, lists and removes cost lines — label, category, amount, who was paid, and a `deductible` flag, since a cost the collective carries whatever happens is real spend but is not the act's to share. `expensesCents` reads that cost sheet rather than the ledger's `spent` rows, which also hold the guarantee top-ups `netCents` already subtracts. Guarded `event.manage`, not `finance.refund`: booking the engineer is the producer's own work and nothing leaves the till when the line is written. **The advance checks riders against stock** (#2724): the Advance tab's Requested gear card lists each item an act asks CMC to supply, matched to the inventory catalog by a staff link (`rider_element.inventory_item_id`, `production.run`) or else suggested by category name, with what no category serves listed as not in stock and a shortfall when one act asks for more than is free.
