# Groups

Status: ✅ /staff/groups

Bands, Clubs and Committees are one list at `/staff/groups` with a kind filter (`?kind=club`, `?kind=committee`; bands are the unfiltered list), each kind with its own columns and capability (#2765; `/staff/groups/bands`, `/clubs` and `/committees` are 308s); a row opens the record as a side panel, and every record is at `/staff/groups/[id]` (#2086). The club and committee kinds are the only place one is created, optionally with a first chair appointed in the same step. Programs have no owner: their admins are the **chairs**, any number including none, and staff make or remove a chair from the roster on `/staff/groups/[id]` (audited as `group.role_changed`); join policy, joining instructions and listing visibility; roster with applications apart from members; deactivate/reactivate. Members get `/member/groups` — memberships and discovery on one page — and a tabbed club page, with `/member/groups/{slug}/edit` for its chairs: name, description, photo and join instructions, and deliberately not `joinPolicy` or listing visibility, which stay staff's because the case for free room time rests on staff settling who runs a program and on what terms — `saveBandProfile`, the other writer of `directoryVisibility`, is band-only for the same reason. Public directory at `/groups`, with self-join and apply. Bands are excluded by kind everywhere and keep `/staff/bands` and `/member/bands`. **Announcements** are one implementation on two mounts — the club page's default tab and `/band/{slug}/announcements` — with one `group_announcement` table behind both: owner/admin write a markdown draft, publish as a separate irreversible act that posts it into the group's one Announcements thread in Messages, pin, edit and soft-delete; every member reads, and drafts are withheld server-side rather than filtered in the page. Publishing fans out to the roster in one batched send on the transactional stream plus chunked in-app rows, latched on `notified_at` so the bus redelivering cannot notify twice; the author, muted memberships and deactivated accounts are excluded in the recipient query, and every email carries a link to the per-group mute (`group_member.notifyAnnouncements`), which the global `announcement` preference cannot express. **Sessions** are a program's events: `createGroupEvent()` is the first path outside the staff panel that reserves the room, and it holds it _for the event_ (`bookerType: 'event'`) rather than booking it as the group, so it is free and touches no credit ledger. `event_group` carries which groups' pages an event appears on, distinct from `event_band`'s credit on the bill. **Documents** are a club and committee file store — a band keeps its rider and stage plot in the public `media` slots instead, and `file-service` refuses one outright. One `file` table in a second R2 bucket (`corvmc-private`) that has no custom domain and no `r2.dev` URL, reached through `src/lib/server/private-storage.ts`, which exports no URL-minting function at all. Owner and admin upload through a remote form — eight allowed types, 25MB per file, 250MB and 50 files per group; every member downloads through `/api/files/[id]`, which authorizes against the file's own stored group, streams the body, and answers `private, no-store` + `Vary: Cookie` + a forced attachment with `nosniff`. Removing a document sets `deletedAt` and touches no object; the daily media sweep grew a private-bucket pass that reaps it a week later, object before row. Hard-deleting a group purges its objects first and aborts if any fails, because `file.groupId` cascades and the rows are the only record of the keys A member sets their **own** display name from their roster row — `updateMyGroupMembership`, the counterpart to the chair's edit, which deliberately cannot rename anyone; their position is the chairs' or staff's to set. Staff can also bulk-import a roster from pasted emails or a Zeffy CSV: an existing account is added active with no acceptance step, an unknown address gets the emailed group invitation (one Postmark batch), and a results card lists added / invited / already members / already invited / invalid.
