# Production projects

Status: ✅ Built

Every production is a project, specialised (#1673, `docs/decisions/0011-productions-are-projects.md`). `project.kind` is `'general' \ | 'production'`, and creating a show writes its project, production and committees in one `db.batch`. `project_committee`holds (project, group, role): Booking takes part in a show as`booking`and Production as`production`, and a committee's `'owned'`grants reach every project it takes part in. Production guards moved off`event.manage`onto`production.book`(acts, offers, deals, billing, poster art, cancelling) and`production.run`(run of show, advance, crew, door, expenses, settlement), both held through the show's project.`requireProjectCommittee`reads the project off the record being written, never off the request. Staff hold both.`production.create`is org-wide and held by Booking, so Booking opens its own shows: **New show** on its committee page's Projects tab runs`createShow`and lands on the console, which either half reads through the project, without`event.read`. Cancelling a show tells its committees who cancelled it (see Committee deliverables). The console shows its project's budget against burn, and a show's ticket sales, act payouts and cost sheet count toward that burn. Duty-list entries can anchor to load-in, soundcheck, curfew and load-out, and their shifts re-time when those move. `/staff/tasks/projects` filters by kind. The staff nav gained a Planning section (committees, suggestions, ballots, projects, agreements, annual report), and Clubs moved under People.`project.group_id`is dropped (#1686); ownership is`project_committee` alone.
