# Contacts

Status: ✅ /staff/contacts

Every outside organization CMC works with (#2622): one `organization` row with `party_role` rows (funder, sponsor, contractor, performer, vendor, press, partner, supplier, donor) and its people as `contact` rows, read only through `contact-service.ts` under `contact.read` / `contact.manage` (staff and the Development committee). One table of every organization and every person with no organization, filtered by role, type, search and archived and sorted by name, last activity or next task (#2760); an organization row opens to `contact.read` or one of its roles' own capability, a person row to `contact.read`; an organization page with roles, people and the grants, sponsorships, contractor jobs, purchase orders and market applications that name it, the band an act became, an act's booked events with terms and payment plus its contact-sheet and Claim actions, and a vendor's standing setup; an outside act is a performer organization whose people `/act/{token}` writes; a Possible duplicates page listing people with the same name or phone and organizations with the same website domain or name, merged by hand with an audit entry. An email already on file attaches to that contact rather than making a second. `/api/cron/sweep-contacts` deletes contacts past `retain_until`. Phase 3: tasks link to an organization, person, unit or booking through `work_order_link`, and Related tasks shows them on those pages; organization and person pages (`/staff/contacts/people/[id]`) carry a timeline of logged notes (`activity`), Inbox conversations filed to them from the Inbox's Details panel, audit entries for their grants, sponsorships and jobs, linked tasks marked done, and in-kind gifts, which a `finance.refund` holder records there as an `in_kind` ledger entry with fair value, day and project (#2727); the list shows last activity and next open task. See `docs/decisions/0069-a-party-has-one-timeline.md`. See `docs/decisions/0065-outside-parties-are-organizations-with-roles.md`
