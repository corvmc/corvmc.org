# Moderation appeals

Status: ✅ On /staff/flags/{id}

A member appeals an upheld report against their own post or standing, once, from the notice that explains it: `/member/suggestions/{id}`, `/member/events/{id}/manage`, and the standing banners on `/member/suggestions`, `/member/events` and `/member/account`. They never name a flag — the service resolves it after checking ownership. The appeal renders on `/staff/flags/{id}` under the decision; an **Appeal waiting** filter and a Content Flags nav badge count the pending ones. Deciding is two independent outcomes (content, standing), applied on save — granting _is_ the restore; a listing republishes directly rather than re-entering review. The staffer who upheld the report may grant but never deny (`SelfReviewError`). One appeal per decision (unique index), reopenable by staff; nothing pauses while pending. Every standing now needs a report: `setStanding` requires `flagId`, and `setMemberStanding` files a `content_flag` with `origin = staff_action`. The member is emailed the verdict only; the staffer's reason stays internal. Bans and account actions are not appealable here. See `docs/decisions/0039-moderation-appeals-hang-off-the-upheld-report.md`
