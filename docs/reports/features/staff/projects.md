# Projects

Status: ✅ /staff/tasks

A body of work with a budget and an owner — the container work orders (and the contractor jobs engaged on them), purchase orders, acquisitions and events hang off by a nullable `project_id`. Events point at projects and never the reverse, so one shape serves a facility improvement (no events), a produced show (one) and a festival (many). A festival can also be a parent project over its show projects (`parent_project_id`, one level, #2543): each night keeps its own production, the parent's burn sums its children's with its own, and its budget page shows spend by project. `groupId` is the owning committee, enforced in the service because a CHECK cannot cross tables; `suggestionId` closes the member loop, with a partial unique index for one project per suggestion, and a status change writes both in one `db.batch`. **Burn is derived, never stored** — summed over the ledgers that already hold the atoms and returned as two columns that are never added: cash that left the account, and contributed value. Contributed is itself **two figures that are never added to each other**: impact value over every approved hour at `volunteer.hourValueCents`, and recognizable contributed services over specialized hours only, each at its role's own `market_rate_cents` — they overlap, since a donated engineer's hour is in both, so the DTO carries no combined total. A specialized role with no rate counts as zero and is reported as a gap, never valued at the impact rate. Donated contractor work (`is_donated` + `fair_value_cents`) is contributed, not cash. `stock_movement` is not a source; it carries a quantity and no value. A member's suggestion can be answered by a project: staff start one from `/staff/suggestions/[id]`, both move to Planned in a single write, and the author sees on their own suggestion that the idea became work — a name and a status, never a budget. A committee reads its own projects on `/member/groups/{slug}`, guarded by `requireGroupRole` rather than by a role, which is how a committee gets a window onto its work without the whole staff panel. An active member can change a project's status there, through `requireCommitteeMember`, which resolves the committee from the project's own `groupId`. Each committee power needs the owning committee's grant list to carry its capability (`project.manage`, `volunteer.manageShifts` for work orders, `volunteer.manageRecurring` for recurring work, `event.publish`, `event.manage` or `finance.read`, all with `'owned'` reach), and the same capability is the staff cover. The page shows only the controls the committee's grants allow. Staff tick these on the group page. A member can also open an unscheduled work order on one, through the same guard with `volunteer.manageShifts` as cover. The same guard lets a member publish a draft event on one of those projects, resolving the committee through `event.projectId`, with `event.publish` as cover. Its Numbers tab shows the committee's cut of `/staff/reports` to its own roster (#1562): each project's budget against cash committed, ledger totals for entries tagged to its projects, and approved hours logged to the committee, through `requireCommitteeMember` with `finance.read` as cover. Its work orders are its tasks (`docs/decisions/0054-a-work-order-is-the-task.md`). Every task, in a project or not, is a row of the Tasks table at `/staff/tasks` (grouped by due date or by project, with Unassigned, Overdue, No set time and Awaiting invoice chips, #2625) and has a page of its own at `/staff/tasks/[id]` (roster, shifts, assignees, engagements, hours, equipment reports, feedback, duties). The project page at `/staff/tasks/projects/[id]` keeps what only a project has, description, budget and burn, committees and dates, and lists its tasks as the same table filtered to it, with New task in that project; the table groups a project's tasks by its sections when it has any (#2653), in section order with No section last, and sections are added, renamed, moved and deleted from their group rows, while a task's page names its section and changes it; the old project, task, `?task=` panel and shift URLs redirect to these pages; a task can wait on others in its project (`work_order_dependency`, cycles refused on write, `docs/decisions/0068-a-task-can-wait-on-other-tasks.md`): Blocked is derived on read and is a chip, a dimmed row and a "Waits on" line on the table, the task page lists Waits on and Needed for, marking a blocked task done warns and is recorded, the last blocker closing notifies the waiting task's assignees, templates carry dependencies onto the tasks they stamp, and the project page draws its tasks as a layered graph; the project's own fields change only through Edit project, and Budget is a tab of its own. An Activity tab reads the project's history newest first: audit entries on the project and on its work orders (written from the shared services, so the shift page and the project page record alike), duty ticks, contractor engagement steps, and the origin chain from suggestion through ballot to the project starting, each entry linking to its task; the task page and the shift page show their own work order's list.
