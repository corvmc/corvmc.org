# Rehearsal orientation

Status: ✅ Setting

A member's first rehearsal booking raises a shift for somebody to meet them at the door. **Since #2034 this is a setting, not a duty list:** Volunteering settings → Orientation names the role and the length (`volunteer.orientationRoleId`, `volunteer.orientationMinutes`; no role means off), and `raiseOrientationShift` inserts one work order at the booking's start, in no project, marked `work_order.is_orientation`, or reuses the live one already there. The history below describes the retired duty-list mechanism. `duty_list` gained a `subject` (`event` \ | `reservation`) and an `auto_apply_on` trigger, and `applyDutyList` takes a `DutySubject` union rather than an event id — a booking and a show are both a window with a start and an end, which is all an offset needs, so one apply and one output serve both. `work_order` gained `reservation_id` as a fourth anchor beside event/asset/project. `reservation.created` is emitted from `create()`/`staffCreate()` (after the post-insert race re-check, so a compensated-away booking never announces itself) and `orientation-listener.ts` applies the list when the booking is a member's and their first. **Idempotence is the existing re-apply guard** — the bus has no dedupe, so a re-delivered event hits the same check that stops a coordinator double-clicking Apply. Cancelling the booking stands the shift down through `cancelShift`, leaving it in the staff notify list with its un-notified count, because telling the volunteer is still a person's decision; the cascade keys on the reservation, so rebooking needs no special case. `member_orientation` stores timestamps and derives `pending`/`scheduled`/`completed`/`waived` — a stored status would stick at `scheduled` for ever, since an orientation nobody claims emits no completion event. Staff see it on the member record's Space tab (with a waive action, reason required) and beside the first-visit badge on the booking page; unfilled ones ride the existing `ShortStaffedCard`. Who may run one is the ordinary clearance gate. Fixed on the way: `isFirstReservationSql` counted a `waitlisted` row as prior history. **Since #2565** completing or waiving it grants the Practice space clearance, and a member without that clearance books their own slot 3 days ahead (`reservation.newMemberMinAdvanceMinutes`); the picker hides earlier starts and says why. **Since #2567 and #2569** the day before, staff holding `volunteer.manageShifts` get an alert for an orientation shift with no volunteer signed up, and the member gets a first-visit email (arrival time, address, door code timing, and staff notes from `volunteer.firstVisitNotes`) in place of the reservation reminder
