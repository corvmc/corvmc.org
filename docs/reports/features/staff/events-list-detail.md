# Events list + detail

Status: ✅ Full CRUD

Two staff surfaces over one table, split by what staff do with a row. **Calendar** (`/staff/events`) is the staff's view of the public gig guide — every source, public statuses + `pending_review`, forward from today — where listings are moderated; its Posted by column names the accountable party (band chip, member, or plain CMC) and replaces the old Source column. **Productions** (`/staff/productions`, its own list again since #3112, after a spell as a view on Events from #2766) lists every `production` row from draft to closed, listed or not: the production's status, the event's status, and the venue, filterable by production status and short-of-acts. Each row opens its listing; a listing with a production shows its status on the Calendar, linking to the console. The `Productions` **page** is not the `Productions` **module** two rows down — that one adds run-of-show and settlement behind each show. The detail view is one page (#3270): `/staff/events/[id]`, at the address `entity-href` resolves every event ref to, with a fixed header (status, date line, "Posted by", the next step, Share with Download QR, and a More menu for Repeat / Unpublish / Cancel / Delete), a sidebar on every tab (portrait poster with Clear / Download or Upload / template; a CMC show's Crew — lead and shifts, each the person or Assign — and its open Tasks with View project), and tabs: Listing, Production for a CMC show, Vendors for a market, History. It opens on Listing, as does a newly created event. Listing is one always-open form with one Save (#3271) through `updateEvent`: Basics, When, Where (practice space, a saved venue or somewhere else), Performers (a community or band listing's bill; a CMC show's is on Production) and Tickets (none, a link, or through CMC). "Somewhere else" is derived from a blank venue plus a typed place, not stored, and choosing Practice space does not hold the room: Hold from and Hold until start blank and the room is held only when both are filled (not a default hold on every Save, because that would create holds on old shows, #3271). A held room shows its reservation, "Held · no conflicts", and **Release hold**, which is staged and written on Save. Moving Hold from or Hold until moves the row in place and keeps its door code. A conflict shows a **Book anyway** button, and Save stays disabled until it is pressed; the override covers that window only. Re-timing the show past the hold raises **Rebook the hold**, which copies the show's times into the window. A saved venue or somewhere else holds no room, so Save releases the hold the show has. A cancelled event is read-only without `event.manage`. The series line, ticket sales, door sales and recap sit below the form, each with its own form; `/production` and `/vendors` redirect to their tabs. The two-hour window of what else is on became a "Possible duplicate" warning in a community listing's Approve dialog, and the page's Campaigns card and production summary card are gone. See `docs/development/business-workflows.md#5-events-and-tickets`. Staff delete for rows that should never have existed (test events, duplicates, spam) — refused once any ticket exists, since cancel is the end state for those; releases the space booking, releases the poster and clears dangling flags. Recurring CMC events: weekly/biweekly/monthly series materialize independent draft occurrences (all sharing the prototype's poster — one R2 object, an attachment each, see `docs/architecture/domain-model.md#already-unified-references`) via the daily generation cron; an occurrence reserves space only when the prototype holds a reservation, since its setup/teardown padding is derived from the prototype's; series badge + cancel on the detail page
