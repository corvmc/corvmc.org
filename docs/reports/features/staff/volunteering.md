# Volunteering

Status: ✅ /staff/volunteering

The coordinator's worklist is **Volunteering** (#2625; `/staff/volunteer`, `/staff/volunteer/shifts` and `/staff/volunteer/schedule` 308 there): volunteer shifts by date, 14 days ahead by default, with count chips that filter them to claims awaiting confirmation, short-staffed shifts and tasks whose shifts have ended, and a link to clearances lapsing before a booked shift, a `?lapsing=1` filter on the Members volunteer filter; each is hidden at zero. A short-staffed shift opens the people interested in its role beneath it, ranked and flagged, to invite. Its badge counts short-staffed shifts in the next 7 days and ended shifts not closed, by the chips' own predicate (`src/lib/utils/shift-staffing.ts`). Hour logs that need a look are in the staff review feed (#2623); member hours count on entry, and only a missing clearance, a specialized-skill role or an entry over 8 hours waits (#1943). Tasks with no shift, assignment and contractor work are on Tasks. The 2026 redesign collapsed seven nav rows into four screens plus a read-only Report: **Schedule** absorbed the shift catalog (its "Everything" window is the old Include-past checkbox, in the page that groups by day); **People** absorbed the volunteers index, the under-18 queue and the clearances table into three tabs, each roster row carrying exactly one action — Confirm, Chase, or Log Hours; **Roles** (once Setup, reached from the Schedule's header rather than the sidebar) absorbed roles and certifications, since a role requires a clearance and a clearance exists because a role requires it. Retired routes are 308s, and role detail survives as an unlisted page; the full filterable hour queue is Volunteering's **Hour logs** at `/staff/volunteering/hours` (#2765), and the by-month/by-member report is under Reports at `/staff/reports/volunteering` (#2075). A shift can also be **given a name** (`work_order.title`) — a heading resolved title → event title → role by `shiftLabel()`, so two work parties on one Saturday stop reading identically on the claim board, and a duplicate carries the name forward where it deliberately drops the event. **Shift detail** is two columns: who is on it, and who to ask — the shortlist moved off the role's page and is judged as of the shift's own date, with three scopes and one flag line per candidate in priority order (missing clearance blocks; lapsing, day-mismatch and history only inform). A **called-off shift becomes a notify list**: `cancelShift` always left its signups in place, and `Notify all` is now the button that tells them, with `notified_at` recording how far staff have got — cancelling notifies nobody on its own, because calling a shift off and telling six people are two decisions. On the member side the dashboard is a **next-action stack beside a claim board**: hours owed, the shifts you are on drawn as a two-step **Claimed → Booked** rail (the state that earns no reminder and never auto-completes, finally legible), then three summary rows; the board filters to the roles you picked with `Show all` one press away. Hours moved to their own screen where a returned log is tinted, carries the staff reason in full and offers `Fix it`. **The shift itself is a page now** (`/member/volunteer/shifts/[signupId]`): the checklist a duty list writes was behind `volunteer.manageShifts` and the door list behind `/staff`, so the crew doing the work could tick nothing and the person on the door could check nobody in. Both are authorised **by assignment rather than by capability** — the signup id names one member and one work order together, the join is made in SQL, and a cancelled signup or a called-off shift stops being one. The volunteer's door list is deliberately narrower than the staff screen: name and code, no email, and cancelling a ticket stays with staff. Underneath, unchanged: staff-defined roles, a standing interest list, shift scheduling with claim/confirm/no-show, certifications gating claims as of the shift date, an hour-log approval queue, a post-shift feedback rollup, and a date-ranged report — which now also prices that time two ways, breaks it down **by committee or club**, and exports it. A log can name the program it was given to (`volunteer_hour_log.group_id`), validated against the committees and clubs the member is actually on — a band is excluded, being its own members' business rather than volunteering for CMC. One `Program Work` role replaced the six per-committee roles, so a committee never becomes a second row that drifts from the group of the same name. **Impact value** covers every approved hour at the Independent Sector rate; **contributed services** covers only roles marked as a specialized skill, at each role's own market rate, which is the narrower figure a financial statement can recognise. The page shows them side by side and says outright that they are not parts of one total, because the specialized hours are counted in both. A specialized role nobody has priced is flagged rather than valued at the impact rate. `/staff/reports/volunteering/export` is the app's first CSV: formula-escaped by default, BOM-led so Excel reads it as UTF-8, carrying the rate and its provenance in the file, with two value columns, blanks rather than zeroes for unpriced work, and no footer total. Staff can **invite** somebody instead of adding them — a chase that holds no place, so the shift keeps reading short-staffed until they answer, and you may ask more people than the shift has room for. Accepting lands `claimed`, not `confirmed`: saying yes is the member's act and booking is the coordinator's. A refusal is kept rather than deleted, so the same person is not chased twice, and it does not stop them claiming the shift themselves later. Expiry is derived, not stored — an invitation dies when the shift starts, is called off, or fills. Clearance **warns** on an invite where it refuses a claim, because inviting somebody is often how they end up cleared. Staff can also assign to a shift (lands confirmed, clearance gate still refuses by name), release somebody (a cancellation, distinct from a no-show), confirm a whole shift at once, and log hours on a member's behalf (backdate window lifted, lands approved and attributed).
