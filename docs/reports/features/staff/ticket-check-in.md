# Ticket check-in

Status: ✅ Staff check-in

Search + check in tickets at the door. Till counts: both check-in screens carry a Till counts card for anyone holding `finance.collect` on the show. It records the cash in the till at a handoff (who and when), lists the show's counts with a total, and lets the counter remove their own. A role with check-in but not `finance.collect` does not see it (#3273).
