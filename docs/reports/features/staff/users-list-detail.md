# Users list + detail

Status: ✅ Full CRUD

List under an org-wide totals strip (accounts, active, sustaining, deactivated or banned; unaffected by the filters), plus a tabbed member record at `/staff/members/[id]` — a full cross-section of one member's participation across every program. Identity header (avatar, name, contact line, bands as links) + a four-stat scoreboard over eight tabs (Overview · Space · Bands · Volunteer · Money · Comms · Moderation · Account), addressable as `?tab=`; the bar collapses to a menu below `md`. Overview carries a derived Needs-attention list; the rest are read-only summaries that link out to each program's own page. First paint is two queries (`getUser` + `getUserOverview`); every other panel fetches on first open and is kept mounted. Includes the app's only user → bands navigation. Role editing, credit adjustment (admins unbounded; staff comp up to 2 hrs of credits, logged as `staff_comp`), certification grant/revoke, soft-delete deactivate/reactivate (cancels future reservations), a ban or a suspension for a term (deactivation plus an `account_removal` record resting on a report; a suspension cancels only bookings in its term, pauses billing and lifts itself when the term ends; the notice email links to a session-less appeal at `/removed/[token]`), an Account removals history card, an email blocklist at `/staff/moderation/blocklist` that refuses sign-up + hard purge for deactivated users (guards owned bands). Standings and reports are read-only on the Moderation tab — a standing is set by an upheld report and lifted through the appeal workflow, not by hand from a member's record.
