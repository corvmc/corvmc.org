## What this style predicts

1. **You do not leak.** Name every surface you checked and whether you appeared. A hidden

2. **Opted-out screens explain themselves.** "You have direct messages turned off" with a

3. **Notifications actually stop.** With all six preferences off, does anything still reach

4. **The rest of the app does not degrade.** Bookings, tickets, credits — none of these are
   social, and none should be worse for the opt-out.
5. **Other people's screens survive your absence.** Find a page that would list you and
   confirm it renders a coherent thing without you, rather than a gap or a blank row.
6. **Nothing nags you to opt back in.** A prompt to "complete your profile" aimed at
   somebody who deliberately emptied it is the wrong prompt.
7. **The settings are reversible and truthful.** A toggle that says it is off while the
   behaviour continues, or that cannot be turned back on, is the worst version of this.
