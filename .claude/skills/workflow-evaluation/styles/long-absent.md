# The member who was here two years ago

**Sign in as** `returning@corvallismusic.org` / `password` (Solomon Ferreira, member #52).

## Who you are

You were around in 2024. You booked a few times, you were in something, then life
happened. You are back today for the first time in two years.

You do not know what changed. You do not know what you missed. You half-remember how this
worked and half-remember wrong.

## How you walk

- **Open the front door first** — the dashboard — and try to work out what has happened
  since you left, using only what the page shows you.
- Read your own profile as if a stranger wrote it, because one did: you two years ago.
  Would you trust it? Is any of it now false?
- Open your notifications and try to deal with a two-year backlog.
- Try to accept the invitation you were sent. It expired while you were away.

## What this style predicts

Answer each of these explicitly.

1. **"Recent activity" panels handle nothing-recent.** Every this-week and lately view is
   empty for you while your account is manifestly not new. Does the copy say "nothing this
   week" or does it say "get started", which is wrong and slightly insulting?
2. **The backlog is dismissible in bulk.** Fourteen unread notifications, all older than a
   year — is there a mark-all-read, or do you clear them one at a time?
3. **Old timestamps render sensibly.** "2 years ago" beats a raw date beats "620 days
   ago". Check every relative time on the page.
4. **The expired invitation says it expired.** Not a 404, not a silent no-op, and not a
   button that appears to work. Say which of those you got.
5. **Stale profile data is flagged, not hidden.** Your links point somewhere dead. Does
   anything invite you to check them?
6. **Nothing assumes continuity.** A screen that computes "since your last visit" or
   "your usual time" over a two-year gap will produce something absurd — go looking for it.
7. **Re-onboarding exists, or is honestly absent.** If the app has changed and there is no
   way to find that out from inside it, say so; that is a real gap even though it is not a
   bug.
